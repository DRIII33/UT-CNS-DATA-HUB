# UT-CNS-DATA-HUB: Business Requirements Document

## 1. Project Purpose
The UT College of Natural Sciences (CNS) requires a unified Data Hub to integrate fragmented data from decentralized systems (SIS, Workday, Research Admin). This platform enables data-driven decision-making while ensuring FERPA compliance for over 13,000 students.

## 2. Functional Requirements
- **Enrollment Analytics:** Track student retention, GPA trends, and graduation rates across different majors.
- **Research ROI:** Monitor grant funding impact by analyzing the relationship between award amounts and publication outputs.
- **Faculty Productivity:** Benchmarking faculty performance based on grant acquisition and student course ratings.
- **Compliance:** Mask all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to meet university data governance standards.

## 3. Use Cases
- **Academic Deans:** Identify 'At-Risk' student cohorts with low GPAs for targeted intervention.
- **Research Office:** Evaluate which funding agencies yield the highest citation counts per dollar awarded.
- **HR/Department Heads:** Analyze faculty salary distributions versus research productivity scores.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Student Success:** Average GPA by Major and Semester-over-Semester retention.
- **Financial Health:** Total Grant Award Amount and Departmental Budget Allocation.
- **Operational Excellence:** Pipeline Data Freshness and 0% Orphaned Record Rate in Fact tables.
